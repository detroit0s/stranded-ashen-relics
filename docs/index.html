A dead world. Seven sleeping gods. One way off.

The colony-ship Perdita fell out of the dark and broke on Karush, a red world of ash and bone under a sun that never sets. You are the Wayfarer, the last of the crew still breathing.

The ship's core is shattered, and the only power left on Karush beats inside the Relic Guardians: gods the Dustborn bound into machines a thousand years ago. Wake them. Take their hearts. And learn why the Dustborn chose to be unmade rather than face what they built.

Installing

  • Windows: unzip the whole folder first, then run STRANDED.exe. If SmartScreen appears, click More info ▸ Run anyway.
  • macOS: unzip, then the first time right-click STRANDED.app ▸ Open, then Open again.
  • Android: open the APK on your phone and allow installing when asked. A new version installs over the old one.
  • Your save carries over from one version to the next, and the game tells you on its title screen when a new version is out.
